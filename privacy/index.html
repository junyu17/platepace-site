Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

PlatePace helps users log meals, hydration, training, medication schedule notes, side effects, and doctor-ready exports. This policy explains what information the app may collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address and display name.
  • Meal logs, food photos or uploads, corrected ingredients, common foods, grocery lists, and nutrition estimates.
  • Hydration, weight, training, medication log, and side-effect entries that users choose to add.
  • Subscription status and purchase validation information from Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Optional Health app or Health Connect data only after the user grants permission.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide meal and training guidance, save user logs, enforce subscription limits, generate user-requested doctor exports, improve app reliability, and provide support.

Food Photos and AI Recognition

When users submit a food photo for recognition, the image may be sent to PlatePace servers and an AI vision provider to estimate food items and portions. Users should review and correct the result before saving.

Health and Medical Boundary

PlatePace is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or recommend dose changes. Side-effect and medication logs are user records that can be exported for discussion with a clinician.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as cloud hosting, AI recognition, purchase validation, analytics, crash reporting, and customer support. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety and security.

User Controls

Users can edit or delete app records in the app where supported, revoke health permissions in system settings, and request account deletion or support by emailing billy.yu@me.com.

Children

PlatePace is not intended for children under 13. Family meal planning features are for adult household planning and do not create child accounts.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Jun Yu at billy.yu@me.com.
